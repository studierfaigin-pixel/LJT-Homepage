## Biography

I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Previously, I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship.

## Research Interests & Skills

My research focuses on Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning. Specific research interests and skills include:
* **Natural Language Processing**
* **Machine Learning**
* **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
* **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
* **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
